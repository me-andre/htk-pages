One source of truth

A sold unit is sold everywhere.

The 3D picker, the plan, the table, the unit page and the PDFs all read the same records. Mark a unit sold once, and it can never stay clickable in the 3D view while the table lists it as unavailable.

On 6 of the 16 developer storefronts we studied that use a 3D view, the view and the units list disagreed.

Compliance built in

The disclosures the law asks for, generated from your data.

Price transparency, consent records and image labelling are part of the platform, not a checklist you keep by hand.

  • Price history and the 30-day lowest price

    Every price change is dated. Every reduction shows the lowest price of the 30 days before it, on cards, tables, 3D views and unit pages.

  • Statutory price lists

    A dated price list for each offering, generated from the prices you enter, parking spaces and storage rooms included.

  • Every payment beyond the price

    Mandatory extras and other payments are shown with the price and counted in the total, so the arithmetic always adds up.

  • Consents on record

    Every enquiry keeps the consents its buyer gave, with their exact wording and the time they were given.

  • Honest imagery

    Renders are labelled as visualisations, and images made or retouched by AI say so on the image itself.

  • No fake urgency

    Scarcity and deadlines come from your inventory and your campaign dates. No countdowns, no invented “only 2 left”.

Built around the EU consumer rules and Poland’s 2025 price-transparency act for new homes.
